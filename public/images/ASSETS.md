# AFTR. asset provenance

Original assets generated with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP for this project. Packaging images preserve the generated alpha channel. The assets are concept visualizations, not representations of a verified formula or manufactured product.

- `eclipse.webp`: realistic matte ultraviolet AFTR. gummy jar with black ribbed cap, cream oversized italic brand typography, acid-lime orbital graphic, ECLIPSE / 01. Photographic studio lighting, transparent background, no ingredients, quantity or health claims. Final edit requested removal of all background and preservation of the exact label and jar lighting.
- `volt.webp`: same jar and label layout, acid-lime paper, black AFTR. lettering, ultraviolet orbit and VOLT / 02, transparent background. Derived from the Eclipse reference through the built-in image editor.
- `runner.webp`, `runner-small.webp`: original nighttime editorial photograph of an adult female runner in a concrete underpass; black sportswear, ultraviolet and acid-lime light, slow-shutter city streaks and flash photography; no text or logos.
- `macro.webp`, `macro-small.webp`: original macro studio photograph of three translucent purple rounded gummies on glossy dark glass, tactile texture, ultraviolet reflections and acid-lime rim light; no text or health claims.

Supporting photographs are served from Unsplash, using the image IDs in `src/main.jsx`: a workspace, gym, city, home interior, morning runner and evening city scene. License: https://unsplash.com/license. No reference-site artwork or product imagery is copied.

Brand graphics, typography compositions and the favicon are original code-native 2D artwork.
